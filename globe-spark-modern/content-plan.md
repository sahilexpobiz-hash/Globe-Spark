# GlobeSpark content refinement plan

## Editorial decision

The reference site has a solid service inventory and a clear SEO-led origin story, but its current copy leans on generic agency language and several unverified performance claims. The refined site should keep the useful context while sounding more specific, calmer, and more credible.

| Keep and refine | Remove or soften unless verified |
| --- | --- |
| GlobeSpark name and digital-marketing focus | “Dominate search engines” and “get to the top of Google” |
| SEO, content, link building, social media, and local visibility services | 200+ clients, 5× traffic, 98% retention, 3K+ keywords, 40+ industries |
| Data-led decisions and transparent communication | +320% traffic lift and −58% acquisition cost |
| Four-part workflow: discovery, strategy, execution, reporting | “Free audit worth ₹5,000” and “no strings attached” |
| Team names and roles, if approved by the business | “Usually respond within one business day” unless operationally true |
| Phone, location, and email, with one consistent email address | Unverified city/location expansion promises |

## Chosen positioning

**GlobeSpark makes ambitious businesses easier to find, easier to trust, and easier to choose.** The agency combines search strategy, useful content, social distribution, and practical measurement into a clear marketing system rather than selling isolated activities.

## Voice rules

Headlines should be direct and outcome-aware without promising a guaranteed ranking. Body copy should explain the work in plain language. CTAs should invite a useful next step, such as “Review my growth opportunity,” “Talk through your next move,” or “Request a focused review.” Avoid inflated words such as dominate, guaranteed, explosive, proven, and real results unless a specific approved case study supports them.

## Approved page messaging

The hero should lead with: **“Make your marketing easier to find—and easier to trust.”** The supporting paragraph should describe the combination of search, content, social, and measurement without numerical promises.

The services section should frame the offering as **“A clearer system for turning attention into action.”** Each service description should explain the user benefit instead of listing a tactic alone.

The proof section should be evidence-safe: **“Proof should make the decision easier.”** It should explain that approved case studies, source notes, and context belong there, without inventing ratings, reviews, or outcomes.

The contact offer should become: **“Tell us what you’re trying to improve.”** The form promises a focused review of visibility, messaging, and the next practical opportunity; it does not promise a monetary audit value or guaranteed rankings.

## Source

The reference site supplied by the user: [GlobeSpark – SEO & Digital Marketing Agency](https://globe-spark.vercel.app/).

## Verification notes

The refined messaging was reviewed at desktop and 390px mobile widths. The new hero, service heading, proof language, contact form labels, newsletter heading, and footer CTA remain readable within the existing reference-inspired layout. Longer phrases such as “What are you trying to improve?” and “One partner across the full growth loop” wrap cleanly on mobile without creating horizontal overflow.

## Style review amendments accepted

The expanded visual pass will keep the strong typographic hero, calm editorial rhythm, and proof-before-polish structure while re-aligning the system toward Signal Cobalt #1952FF, warm paper, and graphite. Green/lime will become a supporting highlight rather than the dominant brand signal. Repeating ticker bands will be reduced to one intentional transition, the hero imagery will move away from cosmic spectacle toward annotated marketing intelligence, and proof will gain larger editorial numerals and clearer evidence framing.

Desktop verification after the revision shows the hero now has a clearer display/body type hierarchy, the action color reads as cobalt, the hero image feels more like a diagram/marketing artifact, the proof module has a stronger 01/02/03 evidence model, and the page rhythm is less dominated by repeated ticker bands. The FAQ remains scannable and the quote/newsletter/footer transitions remain intact.

Mobile verification at 390px shows the hero actions stack cleanly, the image card remains readable, cobalt markers maintain contrast, the process steps and FAQ rows stay scannable, the quote form remains usable, and the footer collapses without horizontal overflow.
