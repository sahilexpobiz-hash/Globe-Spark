Inside The Process

What Happens at Each Stage

A closer look at the work behind every step: so you always know what's being done and why.

1

Discovery & Audit

We start by mapping your current reality: technical health, content gaps, backlink profile, and what competitors are doing right. No assumptions: just data.

  • Technical SEO crawl
  • Keyword & intent research
  • Competitor benchmarking
  • Analytics & GSC review
  • Goal & KPI alignment
2

Strategy Planning

Findings turn into a prioritized roadmap. We sequence quick wins first, then build the longer-term plays that compound over time.

  • Content calendar
  • Link building plan
  • On-page blueprint
  • Local SEO map
  • 90-day timeline
3

Execute & Optimize

This is where the work ships: fixes, content, outreach, and paid where needed. We monitor daily and course-correct fast.

  • On-page implementation
  • Content production
  • Authority outreach
  • A/B & CRO testing
  • Weekly monitoring
4

Report & Scale

Transparent monthly reporting ties activity to revenue. We double down on what works and retire what doesn't.

  • Monthly dashboards
  • Rank & traffic tracking
  • Conversion analysis
  • Quarterly strategy review
  • Scale winning plays
